Rationale: Best movement for the upper clavicular head, creating a full "shelf" look. Dumbbells allow a converging pressing arc and deeper stretch without shoulder impingement. 		Bench at 30°–45°. Extremely joint-friendly with zero groin pressure.

Rationale: Continuous isometric adduction (pressing DBs together) creates an intense peak contraction and pump while heavily loading the triceps without joint strain. 		Squeeze dumbbells together continuously throughout rep.
Push-Ups (Strict) General chest, serratus anterior, core 2–3 sets × 12–20 reps Warmup or Burnout Finisher 6.5 Hypertrophy • ~50–60% Chest Mass
Rationale: Closed-chain scapular health builder. Great for blood flow and serratus activation, but limited overload ceiling for pure mass compared to heavy presses. 		Keep hips level; best used as a light warmup or finisher.
